# Pale Blue Systems Foundation

The **Pale Blue Systems Foundation (PBSF)** is an independent, foundation-led organization dedicated to stewarding **open technical standards and interoperable reference implementations** for systems operating in **space, lunar, planetary, and other extreme or delay-tolerant environments**.

As exploration and sustained operations extend beyond Earth orbit, systems must cope with:

- **Propagation delays measured in minutes or hours**  
- **Intermittent connectivity and scheduled access windows**  
- **Multiple independent authorities and governance domains**  
- **Long mission lifespans far exceeding commercial product cycles**  

These conditions break the assumptions of conventional terrestrial networking and coordination paradigms.

PBSF’s mission is to provide the **shared technical foundation and governance** that enables diverse actors — government agencies, commercial operators, research institutions, and open-source communities — to build **interoperable, resilient systems** capable of coordinating reliably under these constraints.

## How PBSF Addresses These Critical Needs

PBSF directly advances the community-identified needs for communication, coordination, and interoperability in space systems through:

### Open, Interoperable Standards

The Foundation develops and maintains **technical specifications** that:

- Define protocols tailored for **high-latency and disrupted environments**  
- Establish addressing, identity, and authority models for **multi-stakeholder coordination**  
- Specify interoperability profiles and compatibility criteria for distributed systems  
- Provide security, reliability, and lifecycle policies aligned with long-duration missions  

These standards enable systems — whether spacecraft, surface habitats, relay networks, or autonomous robotic fleets — to **exchange data and coordinate behaviors predictably** across constrained communication topologies.

---

### Reference Implementations

To demonstrate practical applicability and to seed the ecosystem, PBSF maintains **reference implementations** that:

- Illustrate how specifications work in real systems  
- Serve as clear baselines for implementers across agencies and industry  
- Support interoperability testing across independent development efforts  
- Provide conformance tooling and validation harnesses  

These artifacts help teams build interoperable solutions aligned with standards that address the needs identified by NASA’s shortfall process.

---

### Interoperability and Conformance Support

The Foundation supports ecosystem compatibility with:

- Interoperability test suites and validation tooling  
- Conformance criteria and public compatibility matrices  
- Documentation of expected interoperable behaviors and migration guidance  

This ensures independently developed systems can operate together in mission environments that mirror those identified in NASA’s civil space priorities.

---

## Operating Domains

PBSF’s standards are shaped primarily by the operational realities of:

- **Space & deep-space communications**  
- **Cislunar, lunar, and planetary surface networking**  
- **High-latency, intermittent connectivity environments**  
- **Multi-authority and autonomous coordination domains**  

These domains define the *design grammar* of PBSF’s work and reflect the conditions NASA and the broader space community are preparing to address.

## Governance Model

PBSF follows an **open, RFC-driven governance process** that ensures transparent roles and documented decision records:

- **Stewards** — mission alignment, governance integrity  
- **Maintainers** — repository health, technical review, and releases  
- **Contributors** — proposal authors, implementers, and reviewers  

All decisions are documented, traceable, and linked to published specifications and releases.

---

## Relationship to the Broader Ecosystem

PBSF provides a **shared technical foundation** that enables public agencies, commercial operators, research institutions, and independent developers to build interoperable systems for space and extreme environments.

Its open standards and reference artifacts allow diverse participants to innovate while maintaining **compatibility and predictable coordination** across expanding operational domains.
